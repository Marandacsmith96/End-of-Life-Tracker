About

Pet Quality-of-Life Tracker helps you notice patterns in how your cat or dog is doing near the end of their life, using the HHHHHMM scale (Hurt, Hunger, Hydration, Hygiene, Happiness, Mobility, More good days than bad) developed by Dr. Alice Villalobos.

The app runs entirely on this computer. Nothing is sent anywhere. It is not a medical device and does not make decisions; it helps you and your vet have a clearer conversation.
