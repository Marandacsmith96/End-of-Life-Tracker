Good to know

  • Same Wi-Fi only. The phone has to be on the same home network as this computer; it won't work from elsewhere, and that's on purpose.
  • The computer stays on. The app window on the computer has to be open for the phone to reach it.
  • Windows may ask once. The first time it's switched on, Windows can show a "allow access" box for the app. Choose private networks and allow it.
  • Using a VPN? The address shown may then belong to the VPN rather than your Wi-Fi. Switch the VPN off, reload this page, and try again.
  • You can forget the passcode. Switch phone access off and on again to set a new one. Every phone is signed out when you do.
  • Phones can't restore a backup. Replacing all the data is only possible from this computer.
