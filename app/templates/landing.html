Why this exists

People can't reliably remember the slope of decline. Memory of "how was she last month" is rewritten daily by hope, fear, and tiredness. Short daily observations, kept over time, answer a question no amount of thinking can: is this a decline, a plateau, or a run of bad days?

The app never gives a verdict. It shows the trend and the criteria; you and your veterinarian decide.
